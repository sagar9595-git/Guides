    Quick Reference: INVEST Principles

    I = Independent Can be completed without completing other stories (but aware of dependencies)
    N = Negotiable Details can be negotiated with product owner; not over-specified
    V = Valuable Delivers clear business value to a specific user/segment
    E = Estimable Team can estimate the effort (dev, QA, design)
    S = Small Completable within one iteration (typically 1-3 days)
    T = Testable Acceptance criteria are clear and observable; QA knows how to test

    Key Definitions

    Epic: A large body of work that delivers a measurable business outcome for a specific user segment or organization, completed across multiple iterations.

    Story: A small, independent piece of work that delivers value to a specific persona in one iteration, with clear acceptance criteria.

    Acceptance Criteria: Testable, observable conditions that must be met for a story to be considered "done." Use plain, outcome-focused statements that describe expected behavior and value.

    Study Tips

    • Review the weak-to-strong examples in the Field Guide (flip cards) — pay attention to what changes from weak to strong
    • Read the "What was fixed" sections — these explain the specific problems and how they were corrected
    • Identify the pattern: Business outcomes (not features) + specific personas + measurable metrics + clear scope
    • Practice scoring: Use the rubric to evaluate the examples and internalize the dimensions
